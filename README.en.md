# HEDAX
**Pioneering Intelligence & Trade.**

[فارسی](README.md) · [Brand guide PDF](HEDAX-Brand-Guide-FA.pdf)

HEDAX brings trade, sourcing and business operations together with digital tools. This repository is the reference for its visual and verbal identity.

## Brand reference

The existing [Persian brand guide v2.0](HEDAX-Brand-Guide-FA.pdf) covers brand foundations, logo usage, color, typography, imagery, tone of voice and applications.

| Color | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Dark Carbon | `#1A202C` |
| Tech Blue | `#3182CE` |
| Metallic Silver | `#E2E8F0` |
| Steel Gray | `#4A5568` |

Typography: Vazirmatn for Persian and Montserrat for English. Use the PDF for exact logo and layout rules.
